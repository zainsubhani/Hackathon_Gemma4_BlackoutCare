# 🏥 BlackoutCare — Offline Downtime Copilot for Hospitals

> AI that keeps clinical workflows running when hospital systems go down.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Modern hospitals rely heavily on digital systems such as Electronic Health Records (EHR), clinical decision support tools, and connected workflows to deliver safe and timely care.

During cyberattacks (e.g., ransomware) or IT outages, these systems can fail completely.

When this happens:
- ❌ No access to patient records
- ❌ No clinical decision tools
- ❌ Fragmented workflows (paper/manual)
- ❌ Increased cognitive load on doctors
- ❌ Higher risk of delays, errors, and patient harm

Despite this critical vulnerability, there is **no reliable offline system** designed to maintain safe, structured clinical workflows during downtime.

---

## 💡 Solution

**MedRescue AI** is an **offline, AI-powered downtime copilot** that helps clinicians maintain safe and structured workflows during hospital outages.

Instead of replacing doctors or EHR systems, it provides:

> **Clinical decision support + workflow continuity under uncertainty**

---

## ⚙️ What We Build

### 🧠 1. Offline AI Decision Support
- Runs locally using **Gemma 4**
- Works without internet
- Provides structured, protocol-based recommendations

---

### 📊 2. Downtime Triage System
- Prioritizes patients (Critical / Urgent / Stable)
- Helps doctors make fast decisions under pressure

---

### 📄 3. Protocol → Action Engine
- Converts complex medical protocols into step-by-step actions
- Reduces cognitive load during emergencies

---

### ⚠️ 4. Decision Under Uncertainty
- Handles cases with missing data (unknown patient, no history)
- Suggests safe fallback actions
- Highlights risks clearly

---

### 🧾 5. Structured Downtime Notes
- Generates clear, structured clinical notes
- Useful for handover between shifts

---

### 🔄 6. Recovery & Sync Mode
- Logs all decisions locally
- Exportable when hospital systems recover
